## Description

Seeva is a powerful and modern Medical and Healthcare Service Template. It is built for websites in the Healthcare, Medical, Hospital, and Clinic multi-purpose React Template. Seeva is based on in-depth research into Medical, Medical Care, Doctor, Healthcare, Health Care, Healthcare, Hospital, Medicine, Dental, Dentist, Pharmacy, and Veterinary industries to deliver all needed layouts and blocks.
